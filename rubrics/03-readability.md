# Readability (0–10)

How quickly can a human understand what this code does?

| Score | Observable Evidence |
|---|---|
| 0 | Incomprehensible. Abbreviated names, misleading comments, no structure. |
| 2 | Some descriptive names but most are cryptic. Control flow requires tracing to understand. |
| 4 | Mix of clear and unclear names. Methods are too long or do too many things. |
| 6 | Mostly readable. Occasional abbreviation or ambiguous identifier. |
| 8 | Clear throughout. Every identifier communicates intent. Comments only where why is non-obvious. |
| 10 | Self-documenting. A developer unfamiliar with the project understands each method without external context. |
